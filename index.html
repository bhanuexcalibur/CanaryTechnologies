Sales Enablement Objection Battlecards

Objection 01
“Our luxury guests expect warm human touch, not artificial intelligence.”
Concede
Luxury hospitality will always center around authentic human warmth and attentive staff presence.
Reframe
Canary automates mechanical paperwork so front-of-house teams can spend more face-to-face time curating guest delight.
Proof to Validate
Evaluate guest satisfaction scores and staff review sentiment before and after digital deployment.
Objection 02
“Our existing Property Management System already has a mobile portal.”
Concede
Most legacy PMS suites provide standard check-in features to satisfy basic requirements.
Reframe
Legacy portals often see limited guest engagement due to mandatory app downloads and multi-step friction.
Proof to Validate
Compare guest completion rates on web-based zero-download links versus app store download barriers.
Objection 03
“Hotel teams don't have time to manage another separate operational software.”
Concede
Front-desk turnover makes training complex new interfaces a real operational burden.
Reframe
Canary writes and reads directly from the property management system, fitting existing staff routines.
Proof to Validate
Measure onboarding duration and shift task completion speed across front-desk staff during rollout.
